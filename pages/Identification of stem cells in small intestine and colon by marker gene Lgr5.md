	- find one target gene Lgr5 that isn't expressed in Paneth cells, so might be a target of stem cells
	- I think they do this in a previous paper and with staining? in situ
- Use a LacZ reporter knock in to make a null that's lethal in early neonate
	- can study expression pattern using hets
	- In adult mice restricted to the bottom of crypts and not in the paneth cells
- These are crypt base columnar (CBC) cells and they're cycling roughly once a day
- Generate a cre inducible GFP knock in at the first start codon
	- hets are healthy and fertile
	- GFP pattern is the same as LacZ
- Cross with a R26 lacZ that has a lox block site that gets removed with tamoxifen induced cre
	- Use this to track over time after Cre pulse
	- show that at first lacZ is restricted to the originator cell and that after a few days it's only in above cells
