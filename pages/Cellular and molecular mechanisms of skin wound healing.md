- Skin is complicated but can be mainly separated into 3 layers (2 of which are important)
	- Epidermis, which is on the outside and contains the openings / external components of appendages like hair follicles, sweat glands, sebaceous glands
	- Dermis, which is full of fibroblasts and ECM
- Clot / scab forms first
- This review says that cells migrate to fill the gap, but I'm not sure that this is known to happen in all contexts
	- i.e. migration vs proliferation
